↗ Trading system, live since 2026

A trading system that sells options premium by rule, not by feel.

Vesper scans a watchlist of leveraged ETFs every trading morning, sells cash-secured puts for the handful of strategies that have cleared a pre-registered backtest and paper gate, and manages each position with resting take-profit orders and an assignment guard. Collateral caps scale with the account, a dead-man health check halts new orders if anything goes stale, and every strategy keeps a paper twin so live fills can be judged against what they should have been.

It started as a Polymarket bot and taught me more about measurement than about markets: pre-register the test, cluster the sample, and never promote on a point estimate. Python, FastAPI, React, Postgres, Docker.
